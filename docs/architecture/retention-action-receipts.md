# Retention Action Receipts

PFEM retention action receipts add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention decision approval = authorization to act on a retention decision
retention action receipt    = evidence that the approved retention action happened
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention decision approval:
authorization to act on a retention decision

retention action receipt:
evidence that the approved retention action happened
```

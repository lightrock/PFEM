# Retention Master Ledger Closeout Records

PFEM retention master ledger closeout records add the next retention release boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention master ledger verification receipt = evidence that master ledger refs/digest were checked
retention master ledger closeout record = formal closure of retention master ledger workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text

evidence that master ledger refs/digest were checked
```

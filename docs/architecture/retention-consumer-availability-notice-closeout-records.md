# Retention Consumer Availability Notice Closeout Records

PFEM retention consumer availability notice closeout records add the next retention post-access boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention consumer availability notice verification receipt = evidence that consumer availability notice refs/digest were checked
retention consumer availability notice closeout record = formal closure of retention consumer availability notice workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

evidence that consumer availability notice refs/digest were checked

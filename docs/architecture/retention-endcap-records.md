# Retention Endcap Records

PFEM retention endcap records add the next retention endcap boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention archive anchor closeout record = formal closure of retention archive anchor workflow
retention endcap record = post-closure endcap record
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention archive anchor closeout record:
formal closure of retention archive anchor workflow

retention endcap record:
post-closure endcap record
```

# Retention Distribution Manifest Closeout Records

PFEM retention distribution manifest closeout records add the next retention distribution boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention distribution manifest verification receipt = evidence that distribution manifest refs/digest were checked
retention distribution manifest closeout record = formal closure of retention distribution manifest workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text

evidence that distribution manifest refs/digest were checked

formal closure of retention distribution manifest workflow
```

# Confidence and Quality

PFEM confidence and quality records describe uncertainty without altering raw evidence.

The principle is simple:

```text
Do not pretend all inputs are equally reliable.
Do not bury uncertainty inside prose.
Attach explicit quality assessment records.
```

## Why separate assessment records?

Raw evidence should stay close to what was received.

Quality assessment is interpretation about that evidence or about later records. Keeping it separate preserves the boundary between:

- evidence
- normalized observations
- findings
- rollups
- reviews
- reconciliation
- quality assessments

## Quality assessment answers

```text
Who assessed this?
What records were assessed?
What did the assessment rely on?
What confidence level applies?
What quality flags apply?
What uncertainty should downstream users know?
What use is recommended?
```

## What this is not

Confidence/quality records are not truth by decree. They are not proof of authenticity. They are not legal certification.

They are operational caution labels for a mesh evidence system.

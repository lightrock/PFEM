# Retention Distribution Receipt Closeout Records

PFEM retention distribution receipt closeout records add the next retention access/release boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention distribution receipt verification receipt = evidence that distribution receipt refs/digest were checked
retention distribution receipt closeout record = formal closure of retention distribution receipt workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention distribution receipt verification receipt:
evidence that distribution receipt refs/digest were checked

retention distribution receipt closeout record:
formal closure of retention distribution receipt workflow
```

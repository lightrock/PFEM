# Retention Hold Closeout Records

PFEM retention hold closeout records add the next retention/status boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention hold verification receipt = evidence that hold refs/digest were checked
retention hold closeout record      = formal closure of the hold verification cycle
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention hold verification receipt:
evidence that hold refs/digest were checked

retention hold closeout record:
formal closure of the hold verification cycle
```

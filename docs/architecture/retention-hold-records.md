# Retention Hold Records

PFEM retention hold records add the next retention/status boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention cycle closeout record = formal closure of the retention cycle
retention hold record           = active hold preserving artifacts under the verified retention cycle
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention cycle closeout record:
formal closure of the retention cycle

retention hold record:
active hold preserving artifacts under the verified retention cycle
```

# Retention Publication Completion Records

PFEM retention publication completion records add the next retention publication closeout boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention final publication notice closeout record = formal closure of retention final publication notice workflow
retention publication completion record = publication-completion layer publication completion record
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention final publication notice closeout record:
formal closure of retention final publication notice workflow

retention publication completion record:
publication-completion layer publication completion record
```

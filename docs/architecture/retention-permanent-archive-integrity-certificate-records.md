# Retention Permanent Archive Integrity Certificate Records

PFEM retention permanent archive integrity certificate records add the next permanent-archive continuation boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention permanent archive handoff closeout summary closeout record = formal closure of retention permanent archive handoff closeout summary workflow
retention permanent archive integrity certificate record = permanent-archive-integrity-certificate layer permanent archive integrity certificate record
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention permanent archive handoff closeout summary closeout record:
formal closure of retention permanent archive handoff closeout summary workflow

retention permanent archive integrity certificate record:
permanent-archive-integrity-certificate layer permanent archive integrity certificate record
```

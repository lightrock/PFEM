# Retention Decision Approvals

PFEM retention decision approvals add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention decision record    = actual retention decision
retention decision approval  = authorization to act on that retention decision
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention decision record:
actual retention decision

retention decision approval:
authorization to act on that retention decision
```

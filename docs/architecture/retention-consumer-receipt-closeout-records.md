# Retention Consumer Receipt Closeout Records

PFEM retention consumer receipt closeout records add the next retention access/release boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention consumer receipt verification receipt = evidence that consumer receipt refs/digest were checked
retention consumer receipt closeout record = formal closure of retention consumer receipt workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention consumer receipt verification receipt:
evidence that consumer receipt refs/digest were checked

retention consumer receipt closeout record:
formal closure of retention consumer receipt workflow
```

# Retention Publication Rollup Records

PFEM retention publication rollup records add the next retention access/release boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention consumer receipt closeout record = formal closure of retention consumer receipt workflow
retention publication rollup record = publication-rollup layer publication rollup record
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention consumer receipt closeout record:
formal closure of retention consumer receipt workflow

retention publication rollup record:
publication-rollup layer publication rollup record
```

# Retention Decision Records

PFEM retention decision records add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention review verification receipt = evidence that review refs/recommendation were checked
retention decision record             = actual retention decision made from that verified review
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention review verification receipt:
evidence that review refs/recommendation were checked

retention decision record:
actual retention decision made from that verified review
```

# Retention Policy Compliance Records

PFEM retention policy compliance records add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention ledger verification receipt = evidence that retention ledger was checked
retention policy compliance record    = check that retention state complies with retention policy
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention ledger verification receipt:
evidence that retention ledger was checked

retention policy compliance record:
check that retention state complies with retention policy
```

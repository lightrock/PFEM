# Retention Terminal Policy Attestation Records

PFEM retention terminal policy attestation records add the next terminal-status retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention terminal retrieval certificate closeout record = formal closure of retention terminal retrieval certificate workflow
retention terminal policy attestation record = terminal-policy-attestation layer terminal policy attestation record
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention terminal retrieval certificate closeout record:
formal closure of retention terminal retrieval certificate workflow

retention terminal policy attestation record:
terminal-policy-attestation layer terminal policy attestation record
```

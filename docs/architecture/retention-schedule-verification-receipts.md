# Retention Schedule Verification Receipts

PFEM retention schedule verification receipts add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention schedule record                = scheduled next action/review for the retention obligation
retention schedule verification receipt  = evidence that retention schedule refs/digest were checked
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention schedule record:
scheduled next action/review for the retention obligation

retention schedule verification receipt:
evidence that retention schedule refs/digest were checked
```

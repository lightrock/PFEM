# Archive Chain Records

PFEM archive chain records add the next archive boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
archive closeout record = formal closure of the archive workflow
archive chain record    = linked summary of manifest/receipt/verification/closeout
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
archive closeout record:
formal closure of the archive workflow

archive chain record:
linked summary of manifest/receipt/verification/closeout
```

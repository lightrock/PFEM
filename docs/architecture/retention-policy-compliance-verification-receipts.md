# Retention Policy Compliance Verification Receipts

PFEM retention policy compliance verification receipts add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention policy compliance record               = check against retention policy
retention policy compliance verification receipt = evidence that policy compliance result was checked
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention policy compliance record:
check against retention policy

retention policy compliance verification receipt:
evidence that policy compliance result was checked
```

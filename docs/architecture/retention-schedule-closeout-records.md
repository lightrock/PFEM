# Retention Schedule Closeout Records

PFEM retention schedule closeout records add the next retention/status boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention schedule verification receipt = evidence that retention schedule refs/digest were checked
retention schedule closeout record      = formal closure of the retention schedule workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention schedule verification receipt:
evidence that retention schedule refs/digest were checked

retention schedule closeout record:
formal closure of the retention schedule workflow
```

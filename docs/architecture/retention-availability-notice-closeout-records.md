# Retention Availability Notice Closeout Records

PFEM retention availability notice closeout records add the next retention release-continuation boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention availability notice verification receipt = evidence that availability notice refs/digest were checked
retention availability notice closeout record = formal closure of retention availability notice workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention availability notice verification receipt:
evidence that availability notice refs/digest were checked

retention availability notice closeout record:
formal closure of retention availability notice workflow
```

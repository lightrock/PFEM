# Retention Terminal Policy Attestation Closeout Records

PFEM retention terminal policy attestation closeout records add the next terminal-status retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention terminal policy attestation verification receipt = evidence that terminal policy attestation refs/digest were checked
retention terminal policy attestation closeout record = formal closure of retention terminal policy attestation workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

evidence that terminal policy attestation refs/digest were checked

# Retention Publication Certificate Closeout Records

PFEM retention publication certificate closeout records add the next retention post-access boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention publication certificate verification receipt = evidence that publication certificate refs/digest were checked
retention publication certificate closeout record = formal closure of retention publication certificate workflow
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention publication certificate verification receipt:
evidence that publication certificate refs/digest were checked

retention publication certificate closeout record:
formal closure of retention publication certificate workflow
```

# Retention Master Ledger Records

PFEM retention master ledger records add the next retention release boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention final index closeout record = formal closure of retention final index workflow
retention master ledger record = release-layer master ledger record
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention final index closeout record:
formal closure of retention final index workflow

retention master ledger record:
release-layer master ledger record
```

# Retention Schedule Records

PFEM retention schedule records add the next retention boundary.

The boundary is:

```text
retention obligation verification receipt = evidence that obligation refs/digest were checked
retention schedule record                 = scheduled next action/review for the retention obligation
```

## Why this exists

This keeps one PFEM responsibility separate from the next.

Plain English:

```text
retention obligation verification receipt:
evidence that obligation refs/digest were checked

retention schedule record:
scheduled next action/review for the retention obligation
```

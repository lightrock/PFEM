# Retention Terminal Final Seal Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal final seal records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_final_seal_record_id`
- `terminal_final_seal_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_final_digest_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_final_digest_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_final_seal_state`
- `terminal_final_seal_scope`
- `terminal_final_seal_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_final_seal_ref_digest`
- `terminal_final_seal_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

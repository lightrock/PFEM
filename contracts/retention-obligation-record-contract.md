# Retention Obligation Records Contract

A PFEM retention obligation records record should identify:

- `retention_obligation_record_id`
- `obligation_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_policy_compliance_verification_receipt_id`
- `retention_policy_ref`
- `obligation_state`
- `obligation_scope`
- `obligated_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `obligation_ref_digest`
- `recorded_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Completion Records Contract

A PFEM retention completion records record should identify:

- `retention_completion_record_id`
- `completion_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_closure_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_closure_verification_receipt_id`
- `completion_state`
- `completion_scope`
- `completion_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `completion_ref_digest`
- `completion_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Access Publication Records Contract

A PFEM retention access publication records record should identify:

- `retention_access_publication_record_id`
- `access_publication_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_distribution_manifest_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_distribution_manifest_verification_receipt_id`
- `access_publication_state`
- `access_publication_scope`
- `access_publication_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `access_publication_ref_digest`
- `access_publication_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

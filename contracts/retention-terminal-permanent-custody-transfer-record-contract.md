# Retention Terminal Permanent Custody Transfer Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal permanent custody transfer records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_record_id`
- `terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_state`
- `terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_scope`
- `terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_ref_digest`
- `terminal_permanent_custody_transfer_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Archive Manifest Records Contract

A PFEM archive manifest records record should identify:

- `archive_manifest_record_id`
- `manifest_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `custody_lifecycle_closeout_record_id`
- `manifest_state`
- `archive_scope`
- `manifested_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `manifest_ref_digest`
- `manifested_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

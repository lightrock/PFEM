# Retention Schedule Records Contract

A PFEM retention schedule records record should identify:

- `retention_schedule_record_id`
- `schedule_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_obligation_verification_receipt_id`
- `retention_obligation_record_id`
- `schedule_state`
- `schedule_scope`
- `scheduled_action`
- `scheduled_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `schedule_ref_digest`
- `scheduled_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

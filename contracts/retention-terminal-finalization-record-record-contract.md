# Retention Terminal Finalization Record Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal finalization record records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_finalization_record_record_id`
- `terminal_finalization_record_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_final_seal_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_final_seal_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_finalization_record_state`
- `terminal_finalization_record_scope`
- `terminal_finalization_record_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_finalization_record_ref_digest`
- `terminal_finalization_record_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

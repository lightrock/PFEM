# Retention Hold Records Contract

A PFEM retention hold records record should identify:

- `retention_hold_record_id`
- `hold_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_cycle_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_obligation_verification_receipt_id`
- `hold_state`
- `hold_scope`
- `held_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `hold_ref_digest`
- `recorded_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

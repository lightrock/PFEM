# Restore Closeout Record Contract

A PFEM restore closeout record should identify:

- `restore_closeout_record_id`
- `closeout_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `restore_verification_receipt_id`
- `restore_receipt_id`
- `restore_approval_id`
- `restore_plan_id`
- `recovery_point_id`
- `closeout_state`
- `outcome`
- `closed_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `closed_by_ref`
- `summary`

A restore closeout record is not a restore verification receipt. It records final workflow closure after verification.

# Retention Publication Rollup Records Contract

A PFEM retention publication rollup records record should identify:

- `retention_publication_rollup_record_id`
- `publication_rollup_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_consumer_receipt_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_consumer_receipt_verification_receipt_id`
- `publication_rollup_state`
- `publication_rollup_scope`
- `publication_rollup_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `publication_rollup_ref_digest`
- `publication_rollup_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Master Ledger Records Contract

A PFEM retention master ledger records record should identify:

- `retention_master_ledger_record_id`
- `master_ledger_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_final_index_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_final_index_verification_receipt_id`
- `master_ledger_state`
- `master_ledger_scope`
- `master_ledger_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `master_ledger_ref_digest`
- `master_ledger_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

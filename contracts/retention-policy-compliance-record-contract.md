# Retention Policy Compliance Records Contract

A PFEM retention policy compliance records record should identify:

- `retention_policy_compliance_record_id`
- `compliance_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_ledger_verification_receipt_id`
- `retention_policy_ref`
- `compliance_state`
- `compliance_scope`
- `checked_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `checked_ref_digest`
- `checked_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Publication Completion Records Contract

A PFEM retention publication completion records record should identify:

- `retention_publication_completion_record_id`
- `publication_completion_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_final_publication_notice_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_final_publication_notice_verification_receipt_id`
- `publication_completion_state`
- `publication_completion_scope`
- `publication_completion_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `publication_completion_ref_digest`
- `publication_completion_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

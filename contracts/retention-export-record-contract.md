# Retention Export Records Contract

A PFEM retention export records record should identify:

- `retention_export_record_id`
- `export_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_summary_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_summary_verification_receipt_id`
- `export_state`
- `export_scope`
- `exported_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `export_ref_digest`
- `exported_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

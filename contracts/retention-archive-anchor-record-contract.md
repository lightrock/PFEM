# Retention Archive Anchor Records Contract

A PFEM retention archive anchor records record should identify:

- `retention_archive_anchor_record_id`
- `archive_anchor_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_notarization_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_notarization_verification_receipt_id`
- `archive_anchor_state`
- `archive_anchor_scope`
- `archive_anchor_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `archive_anchor_ref_digest`
- `archive_anchor_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

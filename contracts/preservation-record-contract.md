# Preservation Records Contract

A PFEM preservation records record should identify:

- `preservation_record_id`
- `preservation_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `archive_lifecycle_closeout_record_id`
- `preservation_state`
- `preservation_scope`
- `preserved_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `preserved_ref_digest`
- `recorded_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

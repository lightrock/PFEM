# Retention Terminal Final Closeout Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal final closeout records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_final_closeout_record_id`
- `terminal_final_closeout_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_publication_terminal_status_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_publication_terminal_status_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_final_closeout_state`
- `terminal_final_closeout_scope`
- `terminal_final_closeout_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_final_closeout_ref_digest`
- `terminal_final_closeout_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

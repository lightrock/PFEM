# Custody Record Contract

A PFEM custody record should identify:

- `custody_record_id`
- `custody_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `disposition_receipt_id`
- `disposition_record_id`
- `source_workflow_kind`
- `source_closeout_ref`
- `custody_state`
- `custodian_ref`
- `custody_location_ref`
- `custody_location_kind`
- `custody_scope`
- `held_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `recorded_by_ref`
- `summary`

A custody record is not a disposition receipt. It records where resulting artifacts are held and who is responsible for them.

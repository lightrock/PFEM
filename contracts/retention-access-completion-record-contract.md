# Retention Access Completion Records Contract

A PFEM retention access completion records record should identify:

- `retention_access_completion_record_id`
- `access_completion_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_release_acceptance_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_release_acceptance_verification_receipt_id`
- `access_completion_state`
- `access_completion_scope`
- `access_completion_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `access_completion_ref_digest`
- `access_completion_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

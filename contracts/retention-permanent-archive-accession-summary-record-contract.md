- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_permanent_closure_notice_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_permanent_closure_notice_verification_receipt_id`
- `permanent_archive_accession_summary_state`
- `permanent_archive_accession_summary_scope`
- `permanent_archive_accession_summary_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_accession_summary_ref_digest`
- `permanent_archive_accession_summary_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

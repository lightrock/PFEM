# Retention Decision Records Contract

A PFEM retention decision records record should identify:

- `retention_decision_record_id`
- `decision_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_review_verification_receipt_id`
- `retention_review_record_id`
- `decision_state`
- `decision`
- `decision_scope`
- `decision_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `decision_ref_digest`
- `decided_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

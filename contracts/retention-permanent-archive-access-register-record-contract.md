- `retention_permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_verification_receipt_id`

- `permanent_archive_access_register_scope`
- `permanent_archive_access_register_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_access_register_ref_digest`
- `permanent_archive_access_register_by_ref`

# Reconciliation Contract

A PFEM reconciliation record should identify:

- `reconciliation_id`
- `reconciliation_kind`
- `created_time`
- `created_by_ref`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `decision`
- `result_state`
- `summary`

The subject refs identify what is being reconciled.

The basis refs identify what the reconciliation relied on.

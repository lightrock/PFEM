- `node_id`

- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_audit_snapshot_state`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_audit_snapshot_scope`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_audit_snapshot_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_audit_snapshot_ref_digest`

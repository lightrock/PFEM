# Retention Terminal Finalization Audit Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal finalization audit records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_finalization_audit_record_id`
- `terminal_finalization_audit_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_finalization_record_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_finalization_record_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_finalization_audit_state`
- `terminal_finalization_audit_scope`
- `terminal_finalization_audit_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_finalization_audit_ref_digest`
- `terminal_finalization_audit_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

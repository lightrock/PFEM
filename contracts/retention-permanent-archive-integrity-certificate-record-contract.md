# Retention Permanent Archive Integrity Certificate Records Contract

A PFEM retention permanent archive integrity certificate records record should identify:

- `retention_permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_record_id`
- `permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_handoff_closeout_summary_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_handoff_closeout_summary_verification_receipt_id`
- `permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_state`
- `permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_scope`
- `permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_ref_digest`
- `permanent_archive_integrity_certificate_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

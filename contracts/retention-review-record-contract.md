# Retention Review Records Contract

A PFEM retention review records record should identify:

- `retention_review_record_id`
- `review_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `preservation_chain_verification_receipt_id`
- `retention_policy_ref`
- `review_state`
- `recommendation`
- `reviewed_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `reviewed_ref_digest`
- `reviewed_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

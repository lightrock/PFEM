# Retention Terminal Permanent Chain Rollup Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal permanent chain rollup records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_record_id`
- `terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_permanent_fixity_register_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_permanent_fixity_register_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_state`
- `terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_scope`
- `terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_ref_digest`
- `terminal_permanent_chain_rollup_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Permanent Archive Terminal Publication Digest Records Contract

A PFEM retention permanent archive terminal publication digest records record should identify:

- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_record_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_publication_index_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_publication_index_verification_receipt_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_state`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_scope`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_ref_digest`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_digest_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

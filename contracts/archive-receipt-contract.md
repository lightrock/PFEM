# Archive Receipts Contract

A PFEM archive receipts record should identify:

- `archive_receipt_id`
- `receipt_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `archive_manifest_record_id`
- `archive_state`
- `archive_location_ref`
- `archived_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `archived_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Confidence and Quality Contract

A PFEM quality assessment should identify:

- `quality_assessment_id`
- `created_time`
- `assessor_ref`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `confidence_level`
- `quality_flags`
- `uncertainty_summary`
- `recommended_use`

Quality assessments should reference known records or artifact paths.

Confidence levels and quality flags must come from `quality/quality-policy.json`.

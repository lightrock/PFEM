# Retention Publication Closure Certificate Records Contract

A PFEM retention publication closure certificate records record should identify:

- `retention_publication_closure_certificate_record_id`
- `publication_closure_certificate_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_publication_completion_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_publication_completion_verification_receipt_id`
- `publication_closure_certificate_state`
- `publication_closure_certificate_scope`
- `publication_closure_certificate_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `publication_closure_certificate_ref_digest`
- `publication_closure_certificate_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Notarization Records Contract

A PFEM retention notarization records record should identify:

- `retention_notarization_record_id`
- `notarization_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_seal_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_seal_verification_receipt_id`
- `notarization_state`
- `notarization_scope`
- `notarization_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `notarization_ref_digest`
- `notarization_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Access Grant Records Contract

A PFEM retention access grant records record should identify:

- `retention_access_grant_record_id`
- `access_grant_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_access_publication_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_access_publication_verification_receipt_id`
- `access_grant_state`
- `access_grant_scope`
- `access_grant_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `access_grant_ref_digest`
- `access_grant_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

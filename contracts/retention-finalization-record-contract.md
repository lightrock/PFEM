# Retention Finalization Records Contract

A PFEM retention finalization records record should identify:

- `retention_finalization_record_id`
- `finalization_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_package_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_package_verification_receipt_id`
- `finalization_state`
- `finalization_scope`
- `finalized_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `finalization_ref_digest`
- `finalized_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

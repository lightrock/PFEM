# Retention Distribution Receipt Records Contract

A PFEM retention distribution receipt records record should identify:

- `retention_distribution_receipt_record_id`
- `distribution_receipt_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_publication_rollup_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_publication_rollup_verification_receipt_id`
- `distribution_receipt_state`
- `distribution_receipt_scope`
- `distribution_receipt_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `distribution_receipt_ref_digest`
- `distribution_receipt_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Terminal Manifest Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal manifest records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_manifest_record_id`
- `terminal_manifest_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_master_ledger_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_master_ledger_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_manifest_state`
- `terminal_manifest_scope`
- `terminal_manifest_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_manifest_ref_digest`
- `terminal_manifest_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

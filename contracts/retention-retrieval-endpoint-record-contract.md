# Retention Retrieval Endpoint Records Contract

A PFEM retention retrieval endpoint records record should identify:

- `retention_retrieval_endpoint_record_id`
- `retrieval_endpoint_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_retrieval_catalog_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_retrieval_catalog_verification_receipt_id`
- `retrieval_endpoint_state`
- `retrieval_endpoint_scope`
- `retrieval_endpoint_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `retrieval_endpoint_ref_digest`
- `retrieval_endpoint_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

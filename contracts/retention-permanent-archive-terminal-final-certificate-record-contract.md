A PFEM retention permanent archive terminal final certificate records record should identify:

- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_record_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_final_digest_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_final_digest_verification_receipt_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_state`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_scope`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_ref_digest`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_final_certificate_by_ref`
- `summary`

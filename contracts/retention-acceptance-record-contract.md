# Retention Acceptance Records Contract

A PFEM retention acceptance records record should identify:

- `retention_acceptance_record_id`
- `acceptance_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_handoff_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_handoff_verification_receipt_id`
- `acceptance_state`
- `acceptance_scope`
- `accepted_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `accepted_ref_digest`
- `accepted_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_authority_notice_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_authority_notice_verification_receipt_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_public_access_certificate_state`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_public_access_certificate_scope`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_public_access_certificate_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_public_access_certificate_ref_digest`

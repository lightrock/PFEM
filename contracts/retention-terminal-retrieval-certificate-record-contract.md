# Retention Terminal Retrieval Certificate Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal retrieval certificate records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_retrieval_certificate_record_id`
- `terminal_retrieval_certificate_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_storage_receipt_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_storage_receipt_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_retrieval_certificate_state`
- `terminal_retrieval_certificate_scope`
- `terminal_retrieval_certificate_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_retrieval_certificate_ref_digest`
- `terminal_retrieval_certificate_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

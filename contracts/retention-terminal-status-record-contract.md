# Retention Terminal Status Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal status records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_status_record_id`
- `status_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_finalization_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_finalization_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_state`
- `retention_status`
- `terminal_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_ref_digest`
- `recorded_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

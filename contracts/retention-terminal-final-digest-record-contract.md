# Retention Terminal Final Digest Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal final digest records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_final_digest_record_id`
- `terminal_final_digest_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_compliance_report_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_compliance_report_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_final_digest_state`
- `terminal_final_digest_scope`
- `terminal_final_digest_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_final_digest_ref_digest`
- `terminal_final_digest_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

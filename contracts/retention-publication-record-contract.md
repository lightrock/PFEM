# Retention Publication Records Contract

A PFEM retention publication records record should identify:

- `retention_publication_record_id`
- `publication_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_report_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_report_verification_receipt_id`
- `publication_state`
- `publication_scope`
- `published_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `published_ref_digest`
- `published_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

# Retention Closure Records Contract

A PFEM retention closure records record should identify:

- `retention_closure_record_id`
- `closure_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_registry_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_certificate_closeout_record_id`
- `closure_state`
- `closure_scope`
- `closure_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `closure_ref_digest`
- `closed_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

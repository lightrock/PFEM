# Retention Endcap Records Contract

A PFEM retention endcap records record should identify:

- `retention_endcap_record_id`
- `endcap_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_archive_anchor_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_archive_anchor_verification_receipt_id`
- `endcap_state`
- `endcap_scope`
- `endcap_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `endcap_ref_digest`
- `endcap_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

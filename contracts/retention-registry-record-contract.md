# Retention Registry Records Contract

A PFEM retention registry records record should identify:

- `retention_registry_record_id`
- `registry_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_certificate_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_certificate_verification_receipt_id`
- `registry_state`
- `registry_scope`
- `registered_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `registry_ref_digest`
- `registered_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

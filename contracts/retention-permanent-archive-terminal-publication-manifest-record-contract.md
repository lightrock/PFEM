# Retention Permanent Archive Terminal Publication Manifest Records Contract

A PFEM retention permanent archive terminal publication manifest records record should identify:

- `retention_permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_record_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_public_release_closeout_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_permanent_archive_public_release_closeout_verification_receipt_id`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_state`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_scope`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_ref_digest`
- `permanent_archive_terminal_publication_manifest_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

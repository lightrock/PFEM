# Retention Seal Records Contract

A PFEM retention seal records record should identify:

- `retention_seal_record_id`
- `seal_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_attestation_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_attestation_verification_receipt_id`
- `seal_state`
- `seal_scope`
- `seal_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `seal_ref_digest`
- `seal_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.

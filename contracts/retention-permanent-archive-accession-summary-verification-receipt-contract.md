- `verification_state`
- `checked_permanent_archive_accession_summary_refs`

- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `expected_permanent_archive_accession_summary_ref_digest`
- `actual_permanent_archive_accession_summary_ref_digest`

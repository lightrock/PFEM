# Retention Terminal Manifest Attestation Records Contract

A PFEM retention terminal manifest attestation records record should identify:

- `retention_terminal_manifest_attestation_record_id`
- `terminal_manifest_attestation_kind`
- `created_time`
- `node_id`
- `retention_terminal_evidence_index_closeout_record_id`
- `retention_terminal_evidence_index_verification_receipt_id`
- `terminal_manifest_attestation_state`
- `terminal_manifest_attestation_scope`
- `terminal_manifest_attestation_refs`
- `subject_refs`
- `basis_refs`
- `digest_algorithm`
- `terminal_manifest_attestation_ref_digest`
- `terminal_manifest_attestation_by_ref`
- `summary`

This record keeps its boundary separate from the preceding PFEM artifact.
